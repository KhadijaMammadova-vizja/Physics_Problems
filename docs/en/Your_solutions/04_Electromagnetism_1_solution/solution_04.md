## 4. Force Comparison

Calculate the electric force and gravitational force between an electron and a proton in a hydrogen atom (average separation \( r \approx 5.3 \times 10^{-11}\,\text{m} \)). Find the ratio \( F_e / F_g \).

---

### Step 1: Write the formulas

Electric force (Coulomb’s law):

$$
F_e = k \frac{e^2}{r^2}
$$

Gravitational force:

$$
F_g = G \frac{m_p m_e}{r^2}
$$

---

### Step 2: Take the ratio

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} = \frac{k e^2}{G m_p m_e}
$$

(Note: \(r^2\) cancels out)

---

### Step 3: Substitute values

- \(k = 8.99 \times 10^9\,\text{N·m}^2/\text{C}^2\)
- \(e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\,\text{C}\)
- \(G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\,\text{N·m}^2/\text{kg}^2\)
- \(m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}\,\text{kg}\)
- \(m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}\,\text{kg}\)

---

### Step 4: Compute

Numerator:

$$
k e^2 = (8.99 \times 10^9)(1.6 \times 10^{-19})^2
= 8.99 \times 10^9 \times 2.56 \times 10^{-38}
\approx 2.30 \times 10^{-28}
$$

Denominator:

$$
G m_p m_e = (6.67 \times 10^{-11})(1.67 \times 10^{-27})(9.11 \times 10^{-31})
\approx 1.01 \times 10^{-67}
$$

---

### Step 5: Final ratio

$$
\frac{F_e}{F_g} = \frac{2.30 \times 10^{-28}}{1.01 \times 10^{-67}}
\approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\frac{F_e}{F_g} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{39}}
$$

**Conclusion:** The electric force is about \(10^{39}\) times stronger than gravity at atomic scales.
