## 2. Electric Potential

Point charges of \(+1\,\text{C}\), \(-2\,\text{C}\), \(+3\,\text{C}\), and \(-4\,\text{C}\) are placed at the corners of a square of side \(1.0\,\text{m}\). Find the electric potential at the center of the square.

### Step 1: Distance from the center to each corner
For a square of side \(a = 1.0\,\text{m}\), the distance from the center to any corner is

\[
r = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1.0}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707\,\text{m}
\]

### Step 2: Use the electric potential formula
Electric potential is a scalar, so potentials add directly:

\[
V = k \sum \frac{q_i}{r}
\]

Since all four charges are the same distance from the center,

\[
V = \frac{k}{r}(q_1 + q_2 + q_3 + q_4)
\]

Substitute the charges:

\[
q_1 + q_2 + q_3 + q_4 = 1 - 2 + 3 - 4 = -2\,\text{C}
\]

So,

\[
V = \frac{(8.99 \times 10^9)(-2)}{0.707}
\]

\[
V \approx -2.54 \times 10^{10}\,\text{V}
\]

### Final Answer

\[
\boxed{V \approx -2.54 \times 10^{10}\,\text{V}}
\]
