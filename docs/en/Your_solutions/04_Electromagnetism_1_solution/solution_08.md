## 8. Magnetic Force on a Current-Carrying Wire

A straight wire of length $L = 0.50\,\text{m}$ carries a current $I = 10\,\text{A}$ in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.20\,\text{T}$. The wire is perpendicular to the field. Find the magnetic force on the wire.

---

### Step 1: Use the formula for magnetic force on a wire

The magnetic force on a straight current-carrying wire is

$$
F = BIL\sin\theta
$$

where:

- $B = 0.20\,\text{T}$
- $I = 10\,\text{A}$
- $L = 0.50\,\text{m}$
- $\theta = 90^\circ$

Since the wire is perpendicular to the field,

$$
\sin 90^\circ = 1
$$

So,

$$
F = BIL
$$

---

### Step 2: Substitute the values

$$
F = (0.20)(10)(0.50)
$$

$$
F = 1.0\,\text{N}
$$

---

### The Answer

$$
\boxed{F = 1.0\,\text{N}}
$$
