## 9. Torque on a Current Loop

A rectangular loop of dimensions $0.10\,\text{m} \times 0.20\,\text{m}$ carries a current $I = 5\,\text{A}$ and is placed in a uniform magnetic field $B = 0.50\,\text{T}$. The plane of the loop makes an angle of $30^\circ$ with the magnetic field. Find the torque acting on the loop.

---

### Step 1: Use the torque formula

The torque on a current loop is given by

$$
\tau = N I A B \sin\theta
$$

where:

- $N = 1$ (single loop)
- $I = 5\,\text{A}$
- $A = \text{area of loop}$
- $B = 0.50\,\text{T}$
- $\theta = 30^\circ$

---

### Step 2: Calculate the area

$$
A = (0.10)(0.20) = 0.020\,\text{m}^2
$$

---

### Step 3: Substitute values

$$
\tau = (1)(5)(0.020)(0.50)\sin 30^\circ
$$

Since

$$
\sin 30^\circ = 0.5
$$

$$
\tau = 5 \times 0.020 \times 0.50 \times 0.5
$$

$$
\tau = 0.025\,\text{N·m}
$$

---

### Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\tau = 2.5 \times 10^{-2}\,\text{N·m}}
$$
