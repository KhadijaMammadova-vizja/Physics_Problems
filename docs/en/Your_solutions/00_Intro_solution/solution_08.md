### Problem 8 – Definite Integrals

Calculate the area under the curve of the function

f(x) = sin(x)

from x = 0 to x = π.

---

Using an integral:

∫ sin(x) dx

The integral of sin(x) = −cos(x)

---
## Step 2
I'll calculate between 0 and π.

So in normal integral the formula is this: F(b)−F(a)

lower llimit= π

upper limit = 0

−cos(π) − (−cos(0))

---

## Step 3

cos(π) = −1
