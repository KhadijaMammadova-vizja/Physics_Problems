# Harmonic Motion Problem

## Problem

A 10 kg mass is attached to a spring and oscillates according to:

$$
x(t) = 0.2 \cos(10\pi t)
$$

Find:
1. The spring constant $k$
2. The total mechanical energy of the system

---

---

## Step 1: Identify Key Quantities

From the equation:

$$
x(t) = A \cos(\omega t)
$$

We identify:

- Amplitude: $A = 0.2 \text{ m}$
- Angular frequency: $\omega = 10\pi \text{ rad/s}$
- Mass: $m = 10 \text{ kg}$

---

## Step 2: Find the Spring Constant ($k$)

For a mass-spring system:

$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}
$$

Solve for $k$:

$$
k = m\omega^2
$$

Substitute values:

$$
k = 10 (10\pi)^2
$$

$$
k = 10 \cdot 100\pi^2 = 1000\pi^2
$$

$$
k \approx 1000 \cdot 9.87 \approx 9.87 \times 10^3 \text{ N/m}
$$

---

## Step 3: Find Total Mechanical Energy

Total energy in SHM:

$$
E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2
$$

Substitute values:

$$
E = \frac{1}{2}(1000\pi^2)(0.2)^2
$$

$$
E = \frac{1}{2}(1000\pi^2)(0.04)
$$

$$
E = 20\pi^2
$$

$$
E \approx 20 \cdot 9.87 \approx 197 \text{ J}
$$

---

## Final Answers

- **Spring constant:**  
  $$
  k = 1000\pi^2 \approx 9.87 \times 10^3 \text{ N/m}
  $$

- **Total mechanical energy:**  
  $$
  E \approx 197 \text{ J}
  $$

---

## Key Concepts

- Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)
- $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$
- $E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2$
