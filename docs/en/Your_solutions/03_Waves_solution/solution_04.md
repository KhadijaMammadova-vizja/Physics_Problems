## 4. Wave Intensity

Wave intensity is proportional to the square of the amplitude:

I ∝ A²

Given:
- Original amplitude = A
- New amplitude = 3A

So the new intensity is:

I' ∝ (3A)² = 9A²

Since the original intensity is proportional to A²:

I' = 9I

### Final Answer:
If the amplitude is tripled, the intensity becomes **9 times greater**.
