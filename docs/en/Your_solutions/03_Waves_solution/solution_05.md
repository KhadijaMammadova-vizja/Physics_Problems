## 5. Echo Ranging

The 1 second delay is the **round-trip time** for the sound:
- from the person to the cliff, and
- back from the cliff to the person.

So the total distance traveled by the sound is:

distance = v × t = 343 × 1 = 343 m

This is for the trip to the cliff **and back**, so the one-way distance is:

d = 343 / 2 = 171.5 m

### The Answer:
The cliff is **171.5 m** away.
