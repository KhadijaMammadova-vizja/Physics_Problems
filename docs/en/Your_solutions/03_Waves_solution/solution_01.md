## 1. Wave Properties

We use the wave relation:

v = fλ  ⇒  λ = v / f

Given:
- Frequency: f = 440 Hz

### In air:
- Speed: v = 343 m/s

λ_air = 343 / 440 ≈ 0.78 m

### In water:
- Speed: v = 1482 m/s

λ_water = 1482 / 440 ≈ 3.37 m

### Final Answer:
- Wavelength in air: **0.78 m**
- Wavelength in water: **3.37 m**
