## 6. Wave Equation

Given:

y(x,t) = 0.05 sin(2πx − 50πt)

Compare with the standard form of a traveling wave:

y(x,t) = A sin(kx − ωt)

So we identify:
- Amplitude: A = 0.05 m
- Wave number: k = 2π rad/m
- Angular frequency: ω = 50π rad/s

### a) Amplitude

The amplitude is the coefficient of the sine function:

A = 0.05 m

### b) Wavelength

Use:

k = 2π / λ

So,

λ = 2π / k = 2π / 2π = 1 m

### c) Frequency

Use:

f = ω / 2π

So,

f = 50π / 2π = 25 Hz

### d) Wave speed

Use:

v = fλ

So,

v = 25 × 1 = 25 m/s

(Equivalently, v = ω / k = 50π / 2π = 25 m/s)

### Final Answer:
- Amplitude: **0.05 m**
- Wavelength: **1 m**
- Frequency: **25 Hz**
- Wave speed: **25 m/s**
